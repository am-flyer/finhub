### 4. Diversification and Budget Guidance

Your current portfolio is concentrated in a few sectors: technology (AVGO, GOOGL, MSFT, NVDA, SPCX), energy (COP, CVX, XOM), and consumer staples/healthcare (JNJ, KO, PG).

*   **Diversification:** While you own strong companies, having a significant portion of your portfolio in a few individual stocks, especially in high-growth sectors like tech, carries **concentration risk**. This means if one sector or company faces a major setback, it could significantly impact your overall portfolio. As you continue investing, consider gradually diversifying into other sectors (e.g., utilities, real estate, broader market index funds/ETFs) to spread your risk.
*   **Budget ($100/month):** Your $100 monthly budget is perfect for consistent investing using fractional shares. This allows you to buy small pieces of even expensive stocks regularly, which is a great way to practice **dollar-cost averaging**. This strategy involves investing a fixed amount regularly, regardless of market fluctuations, which can help reduce the impact of volatility over time.
